1. Executive Summary (Session Overview)

The primary objective of Session 1 is transitioning the cross-functional construction organization (architects, PMs, site supervisors, procurement officers, hospitality operators) from informal conversational chat into a standardized, zero-error engineering workflow:

  • Architecture & Models: Strategic alignment of 2026 frontier models (Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon) based on technical fitness for contracts, calculations, and research.
  • Security & Governance: Enforcing the "Traffic Light" protocol and enterprise Zero Data Retention (ZDR) guarantee to keep corporate and banking data strictly isolated. Autonomous agents must never receive ORBI passwords, banking access, or red category credentials.
  • Engineering Prompting Frameworks: Utilizing C.R.E.A.T.E. and P.A.R.T.S. structured blueprints for official notices, regulatory audits, and on-site reporting.
  • Persistent Asset Assistants: Launching dedicated Claude Projects and Gemini Gems per property (Central Park Towers, ORBI City), with archival long-context RAG (Google Gemini NotebookLM) covered in Session 2.

5. "Traffic Light" Data Security Protocol

🔴 Red Zone (Strictly Prohibited)

Corporate bank account numbers, IBANs, personal national IDs, unexecuted confidential margin sheets, executive passwords. Autonomous AI agents must never receive ORBI passwords, banking access, or red category credentials.

🟡 Yellow Zone (Enterprise ZDR Only)

Architectural room schedules, CAD/PDF drawings, site inspection photos, contractor identities (anonymized to "Contractor A"). Permitted strictly within licensed enterprise accounts under Zero Data Retention.

🟢 Green Zone (Open Public Data)

Georgia Decree No. 41 regulations, public international building codes, standard prompt templates, mathematical engineering formulas. Completely unconstrained for any model tier.

7. V.E.R.I.F.Y. Checklist (Hallucination Control Protocol)

V - Validate Facts Cross-check all factual claims against primary source documentation (executed contracts, verified drawings).
E - Evaluate Sources Confirm AI outputs are rooted in your uploaded files rather than generalized internet training assumptions.
R - Read Details Carefully scrutinize high-stakes stipulations (dates, contractual milestones, liabilities).
I - Inspect Variables Verify units of measurement (m², m³, linear meters, centimeters, millimeters).
F - Fact-Check Math Manually recalculate financial line-item extensions and liquidated damages rates.
Y - Yield with Sign-off No AI-assisted document is ever transmitted externally without formal human engineer approval.
