# ==============================================================================
# ORBI GROUP AI DATA SANITIZATION GUIDE
# ORBI Group · Enterprise AI Security Standard (Zero Data Retention)
# ==============================================================================

Corporate Security & Data Governance Principle:
ORBI Group employees operate under strict Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enterprise licensing, ensuring that no company data is stored or utilized for AI model retraining. Notwithstanding these technical safeguards, upfront data sanitization and masking remain strictly mandatory for all team members.

Data Classification by "Traffic Light" Protocol:

🔴 RED ZONE (STRICTLY PROHIBITED FROM AI INGESTION):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Technical blueprints and digital access configurations for master server rooms, executive suites, and security control centers.
2. Exact camera view cones, dead zones, and IP addressing schemes for CCTV systems.
3. Access credentials, administrative passwords, and security alarm pin codes.
4. Personal identifying information (PII) of employees, buyers, and guests (national identification numbers, personal mobile numbers, payroll ledgers).
5. Corporate bank account numbers, IBANs, and financial credit facility details. Autonomous AI agents must never receive ORBI passwords, banking access, or red category credentials.

🟡 YELLOW ZONE (MANDATORY VARIABLE MASKING WITH ZDR):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Specific asset / tower address ➔ Mask as: [Property_X, Tower_A, 42_Floors]
2. Subcontractor trade names ➔ Mask as: [Contractor_A], [HVAC_Subcontractor]
3. Precise contract lump-sum values ➔ Mask as: [Budget_78K_GEL] or [Contract_Sum]
4. Individual engineer or executive names ➔ Mask as: [Lead_PM], [Site_Supervisor]
5. Agreement numbers and execution dates ➔ Mask as: [Contract_No_N], [Execution_Date]

🟢 GREEN ZONE (OPEN AND UNRESTRICTED FOR AI PROCESSING):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Georgia Government Decree No. 41, national building codes, and safety standards (public domain).
2. Spatial measurements, floor areas, perimeters, ceiling clearances (e.g. 142 m² tile, 48 m² glazing).
3. Generic civil and MEP specifications (porcelain stoneware 60x120, tempered glass 10 mm, LED 4000K).
4. On-site defect descriptions (plaster hairline cracks, curtain wall silicone joint bead, conduit routing).
5. Standard engineering prompt frameworks, briefs, C.R.E.A.T.E. / P.A.R.T.S. templates, and QA checklists.

Practical Data Sanitization Example:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
INCORRECT (🔴 Unmasked / Non-Compliant):
"Subcontractor GeoConstruction LLC is 12 days late on Tower B Level 14 HVAC ductwork installation under contract #CPT-HVAC-2026 valued at 480,000 GEL. Managing director David Kakhidze (ID 01024045892) is not answering calls."

CORRECT (🟢 Sanitized / Compliant):
"At Central Park Towers [Tower_B, Level_14], subcontractor [Contractor_A] has delayed HVAC ductwork installation past the baseline milestone by 12 calendar days under [Contract_No_CPT-HVAC]. Contract value is [Contract_Sum_GEL]. Generate a formal default notice demanding an accelerated Catch-up Schedule within 72 hours."
