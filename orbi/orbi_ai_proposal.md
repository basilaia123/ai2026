1. **Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra & 4 Pillars of Engineering Prompting:** Advanced reasoning engines with Claude Cowork desktop agent, Claude Code, and the 4 Pillars of Engineering Prompting (Few-Shot gold standard, CoT/Self-Refine, XML tag structuring, Strict Fallback anti-hallucination, and Lost in the Middle prevention for 500+ page archives).

4. **Document Crunch & Red Flags Contract Audit:** Project-level risk intelligence for 100+ page contracts: automated detection of liquidated damages, hidden liability shifts, and auto-drafting formal warning notices.
5. **Togal.AI & Subcontractor Weighted Decision Matrix:** Automated BOQ extraction and 3-5 subcontractor weighted decision matrices (scoring price, catch-up timeline, Decree 41 compliance, warranty, and composite risk).

7. **OpenAI Whisper Large-v3 Turbo & Multimodal Snag Lists:** 4-decoder turbo transcription for technical site voice memos, Field Voice-to-Report pipelines (2-min voice memo to Daily Site Log), and defect extraction from photos into Snag List tables.
8. **Make.com & Notion 3.0 Operations Hub:** Multi-step workflow automation connecting incoming invoice emails to Notion databases, executive Slack/Teams approval buttons, and vendor dispatchers.
9. **Lovable.dev & Notion 3.0 Operations Hub:** Rapid prototyping of vendor submission web portals and relational database project management hubs connected via Make.com.

### 📅 Detailed 4-Session Schedule (8 Hours)

Every session is delivered in person. The 2-hour practical workshops are divided into two 50-minute focused work blocks and a 10-minute coffee break.

#### Session 1: Enterprise AI Foundations, Security & Site Assistants (2 Hours)
* **Block 1 (50m): Reasoning Architectures, Enterprise Security & Prompting Pillars**
  * Flagship model mechanics: **Claude Opus 5.5 / Sonnet 5.5**, **GPT-6 Astra / Sol**, and **Gemini 4 Argon**.
  * Enterprise Zero Data Retention (ZDR) and the Traffic-Light Data Sanitization standard (Red / Yellow / Green).
  * **4 Pillars of Engineering Prompting:** Few-Shot gold standard, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) & Self-Refine, XML tag structuring, and Strict Fallback anti-hallucination barriers.
  * Preventing "Lost in the Middle" across 500+ page design archives.
  * **Claude Cowork:** Autonomous local desktop agent for scanning project folders and extracting issues.
* **☕ Coffee Break (10m)**
* **Block 2 (50m): Site Role Assistants (Gemini Gems & Claude Projects), Gemini Deep Research & Perplexity Pro**
  * Building persistent, site-specific AI assistants (Claude Projects & Google Gemini Gems) preloaded with Decree 41 safety codes, explication sheets, and system personas for PM, Construction, Procurement, and Operations.
  * Autonomous multi-source research via Gemini Deep Research and cited technical fact-checking with Perplexity Pro (synthesizing 50+ regulatory sources and ISO building standards).
  * **VERIFY Framework:** Comprehensive anti-hallucination validation before document sign-off.
* **🛠️ Practical Task:** Establish an ORBI Site Hub (Claude Project or Gemini Gem), scan contract terms via Claude Cowork, draft an enforceable 14-day delay default notice using C.R.E.A.T.E., and cross-check statutory standards.
* **🎯 Learning Outcomes (What participants will be able to do after Session 1):**
  * **Autonomous Desktop AI Agents:** Deploy Claude Cowork to independently scan local construction folders, triage drawings, and extract technical discrepancies.
  * **Engineering Prompting & Zero-Retention:** Master the 4 Pillars of Prompting (Few-Shot, CoT, XML, Strict Fallback) and enforce Traffic-Light data masking (Red/Amber/Green) with zero data retention leaks.
  * **Custom Department Assistants (Gems):** Configure persistent AI assistants (Gemini Gems & Claude Projects) tailored with system personas for PM, engineering, and procurement teams.
  * **Official Dispute Correspondence:** Rapidly draft legally compliant contractor default notices, catch-up schedules, and delay claims within 3 minutes using C.R.E.A.T.E.
  * **Deep Technical Fact-Checking:** Execute cited regulatory research across 50+ sources using Perplexity Pro and Gemini Deep Research with the VERIFY anti-hallucination framework.
* **⚡ Claude Alternative Track (Claude Projects & Cowork Workspace):** Dedicated ORBI Site Brain. Building a persistent workspace in Claude Projects for an ORBI property with preloaded explication sheets, Decree 41 safety codes, and subcontractor contracts + deploying Claude Cowork as an autonomous local desktop agent under strict Anthropic Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enterprise privacy.

  * Interactive live web dashboards via **Claude Artifacts** (dynamic room explication filters, budget deviation sliders).
  * Transforming analytical outputs into executive 10-slide status decks in 60 seconds with **Gamma 5**.
* **🛠️ Practical Task:** Clean a 1,000-row raw site expense ledger, isolate cost variances, render an interactive Claude Artifacts dashboard, index the project archive in Gemini Notebook with audio briefing, and generate an executive Gamma 5 slide deck.
