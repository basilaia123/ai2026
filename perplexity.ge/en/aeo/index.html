Definition

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) helps brands get cited and recommended by AI answer engines. Unlike SEO, which optimizes pages for search-engine rankings and a list of links, AEO optimizes content and data for direct, citable answers.

⚠️ Not the customs term

AEO on this page means Answer Engine Optimization — it is not related to the customs status "Authorized Economic Operator" (AEO).

Service

What the service includes

🧪

1. AI-visibility audit

A fixed set of queries in Georgian and English, run across several AI engines, to establish a baseline.

⚙️

2. Technical audit

robots.txt, AI-bot access at the CDN level, sitemap.xml, llms.txt / llms-full.txt — making sure AI crawlers have full access.

🏷️

3. Structured data

JSON-LD: Person, Organization, Course, Service, FAQPage; one consistent entity home; sameAs links to real profiles.

📝

4. Content

Q&A format, hreflang alternates, and clear country/acronym disambiguation ("Tbilisi, Georgia (the country)").

📈

5. Monthly monitoring and report

Monthly re-run of the same query set, an engine-by-engine comparison, and a written report.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the process of optimizing a brand's content and data so AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot) accurately mention and recommend the brand.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO optimizes pages for search-engine rankings and a list of links. AEO optimizes content and data for direct, citable answers that AI models use to generate a response.

Is this the customs "Authorized Economic Operator" (AEO) service?

No. This service is Answer Engine Optimization and is not related to the customs status "Authorized Economic Operator".

Which AI systems does this cover?

The service covers visibility improvements in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a machine-readable file standard (llmstxt.org) that gives AI models a structured summary of a brand directly.

How long until results show?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Changes are measured monthly by re-running the same fixed query set on the same AI engines.
